Honouring a Legacy. Supporting Future Generations.

The Harcharan Singh Benevolent Bursary was established in memory of the late Professor Harcharan Singh (1941–2020), whose leadership helped shape the beginnings of computing education at NTU and widen opportunities for those who followed.

The bursary provides financial assistance and encouragement to CCDS students throughout their academic journey, ensuring that financial circumstances do not stand in the way of talent, potential and perseverance. Through this support, it extends Professor Singh's commitment to education and student welfare, enabling new generations of students to pursue their potential and help shape the future of computing.

About Professor Harcharan Singh

Professor Harcharan Singh was the Founding Dean of the School of Applied Science (SAS), established in 1988. SAS marked the beginnings of computing education at NTU, introducing Singapore's first Bachelor of Applied Science in Computer Technology and welcoming a pioneer degree cohort of approximately 140 students.

The School laid the foundations for what would later evolve into the School of Computer Engineering (SCE), the School of Computer Science and Engineering (SCSE), and eventually the College of Computing and Data Science (CCDS).

Professor Singh's vision, leadership and deep care for students helped shape the early development of NTU computing and the academic community that has grown from it.

Remembering Professor Harcharan Singh

Hear from those who knew Professor Singh as they reflect on his leadership, character, and contributions to the NTU computing community.

Angela Goh

Emeritus Professor, CCDS

"I have had the privilege of working under Prof Singh for nearly 15 years. He was a highly principled person and expected his staff to behave likewise. He wanted us to give of our best for every student... He had great foresight in crafting the curriculum and supporting new areas of research. He will always be remembered as a pioneer at NTU and in Singapore's electronics and IT industry."

Professor Seah Hock Soon

Pioneer Faculty Member

"In 1988, I was one of six pioneers who helped establish the School of Applied Science (SAS) together with our founding Dean, Professor Harcharan Singh... Professor Singh was a stern leader who struck fear in many staff and students, yet he cared deeply about their growth and wellbeing. Working closely with him taught me the value of leading with both discipline and compassion."

Support Future Generations

Every contribution to the Harcharan Singh Benevolent Bursary helps reduce financial barriers for CCDS students, giving them greater confidence to pursue their education and realise their potential.

Led by the CCDS Alumni Association and supported by the College, fundraising initiatives in 2026 and 2027 will contribute towards building the bursary and extending its support to future generations of students.
